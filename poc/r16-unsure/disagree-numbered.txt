#38 r16-0a4385b74c877f63 POST /extend
operationId: extend
summary: Extend the signatures of a PDF
description: Extends every signature of a PDF to PAdES-BASELINE-T, -LT or -LTA. The result is the input PDF followed by an incremental update, so the original signatures stay valid. LT embeds the certificates and revocation data the signatures need; T and LTA add a document timestamp from the server's TSA. A B-level signature extended to LT needs the TSA too: it gets a timestamp first. DSS checks the signatures first and refuses with `EXTENSION_FAILED` when one does not validate, a certificate is revoked or expired, revocation data is missing, every signature is already at a higher level than the target, or a timestamp is needed and no TSA is configured; a TSA that cannot be reached or answers badly is `TSA_FAILED`. Known DSS issue: on a PDF with a signed field whose `/Lock` restricts all fields (FieldMDP `Action=All`), DSS 6.5.1 reports the document timestamp its own LTA extension adds as a change the lock forbids (a `FieldMDP` / `SigFieldLock` warning when the result is validated); the verdict is unaffected.
